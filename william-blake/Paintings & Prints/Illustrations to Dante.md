source: "102 watercolour designs and seven engravings for Dante's Divine Comedy, begun October 1824 at John Linnell's commission and unfinished at his death in August 1827; the seven plates were published by Linnell in 1838. 28 of the 102 watercolours and six of the seven engravings are reproduced here, from Tate, the National Gallery of Victoria, the Fogg Art Museum (Harvard), the British Museum, the Birmingham Museum and Art Gallery, and the Metropolitan Museum of Art, each via its own public-domain release on Wikimedia Commons; the rest, dispersed among these and other collections, are not yet found in an acceptable public-domain reproduction. Watercolours dispersed among Tate, the National Gallery of Victoria, the Fogg Art Museum (Harvard), the Birmingham Museum and Art Gallery, the Ashmolean, the Fitzwilliam, and other collections; the seven engravings at the Metropolitan Museum of Art, the National Gallery of Art, the British Museum, and the Yale Center for British Art. The four engravings reproduced from the Metropolitan Museum of Art are Rogers Fund, 1917, accession 17.65.1, 17.65.2, 17.65.3 and 17.65.5; \"Dante Running from the Three Beasts\" is Butlin 812.1. Every watercolour and engraving here carries its own \"Public domain\" licence tag on Wikimedia Commons, independent of any reuse terms a holding institution states on its own site. Public domain."
