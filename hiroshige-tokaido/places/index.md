> Nihonbashi in Edo to Sanjō Ōhashi in Kyoto: 126 ri, about 495 kilometres, by the Edo-period register.

The map shows the fifty-five places; zoom in on a cluster to separate them, and press the fit button to return. Each place belongs to the prefecture it lies in today, and each prefecture has its own page. Pins are post-station centres taken from the route table of the Japanese Wikipedia article on the Tōkaidō and checked against the individual station articles; they are good to roughly a kilometre.
