# Edo to Kyoto, *station by station*

::: {.eyebrow}
The Road
:::

The Tōkaidō ran 126 ri, about 495 km, along the Pacific side of Honshu. Hover, focus or tap a station to see its print, and press Enter to open it.

On foot it took roughly two weeks. In 1832 Hiroshige is said to have travelled the road with an official procession to Kyoto, and the series is often traced to that trip, though how much he drew on the spot is debated.
