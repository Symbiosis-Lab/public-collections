# Fifty-three Stations of the Tōkaidō
東海道五十三次

Utagawa Hiroshige, 1833–34. Fifty-five prints, from Nihonbashi in Edo to Sanjō Ōhashi in Kyoto.
:::

Each print is one place on the road: a ferry at Kawasaki, snow at Kanbara, a tea stall at Fukuroi. This catalogue puts all fifty-five in the order a traveller met them, each on its own page with its licence, its source and the evidence for both.

Browse by [[Prints|sequence]], on [[places|the road itself]] as a map, or in the [[Browse|index]].

Start at the first print, [[Nihonbashi]], or read [[About]] for where the images come from and what was checked.
