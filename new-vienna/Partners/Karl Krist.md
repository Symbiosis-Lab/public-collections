byline: Architect · 1883–1941

From 1921 he took part in settlement projects and soon counted among the most productive architects of municipal housing, with the Dr.-Franz-Klein-Hof, the Anton-Schrammel-Hof and the Liebknechthof. With Robert Oerley he planned the George-Washington-Hof (1927–1930).
