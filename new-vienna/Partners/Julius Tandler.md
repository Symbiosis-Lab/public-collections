Anatomist, elected to the city council in 1919 and councillor for welfare and health from 1920. Most of the city's welfare and health institutions of the period were set up under him. His 1924 essay on marriage and population policy called for measures against what it called “life unworthy of life”; historians read it as an early socialist eugenics.
