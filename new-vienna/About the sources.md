In 1926 the City of Vienna published its own overview of the municipal programme, *Das Neue Wien. Städtewerk*, four volumes, 1926–1928. This board rebuilds that overview from what today's archives keep: the Wien Geschichte Wiki run by the city's archive, Wikipedia articles that cite the period records, and the Wien Museum's online collection.

## How a project gets on the board

- Every fact on a project page comes from a source listed under it, with the day it was checked.
- The descriptions are written for this board, in our own words. They say no more than the sources do.
- Where sources disagree, the page says so instead of choosing.
- Projects that began before 1919 are on the board when the city carried them on in this period, and their page says when they began.

## Photographs

Photographs from the Wien Museum's online collection, each marked CC0 on its object page, cropped to the print: the museum's scans include the mount and a colour target.

## What is missing

Culture is thin: no counted source was found yet for the Workers' Symphony Concerts or the city libraries. The kindergartens, the adult education centres and the city's libraries are not on the board yet, for want of a dated source.
